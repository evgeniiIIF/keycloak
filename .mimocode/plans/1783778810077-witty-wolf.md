## Why two URLs are needed

| Context | URL | Why |
|---------|-----|-----|
| BFF→Keycloak (server) | `http://keycloak:8080/...` | Docker-internal DNS, only resolvable inside Docker network |
| Browser→Keycloak (redirect) | `http://localhost:8080/...` | Browser runs on host machine, can't resolve `keycloak` |

This is standard for Docker-based OAuth — two network contexts with different addressability.
